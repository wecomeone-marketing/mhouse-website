This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain and protect personal data when a person:

  • visits or uses mhouse.cy;
  • makes or manages a room booking;
  • purchases or reserves pool access;
  • purchases a pass or membership;
  • books a meeting room, private office, work-lounge service or other facility;
  • makes a café, table, event or venue enquiry or reservation;
  • purchases a gift voucher;
  • creates or uses an online account;
  • contacts us by email, telephone, contact form, WhatsApp or social media;
  • visits, stays at or uses M House;
  • uses our parking or guest Wi-Fi;
  • attends an event at M House;
  • receives marketing from us; or
  • applies to work with us.

This policy applies whether the interaction takes place directly through our website, through an integrated booking or payment service, through an application programming interface, or through a third-party platform used to provide an M House service.

2. Adults only

M House is an adults-only property and our website and services are intended only for persons aged 18 or over.

We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal data from anyone under 18 through our website.

A person making a booking confirms that they are at least 18 years old and that every person included in the booking meets the applicable minimum-age requirement for the service booked.

If we discover that personal data has been submitted by or concerning a person under 18 without a valid legal reason, we will delete or restrict that information as appropriate.

Concerns about information relating to a person under 18 should be sent to hello@mhouse.cy.

3. Personal data we collect

The information we collect depends on the service used and the way a person interacts with us.

3.1 Identity and contact information

This may include:

  • full name;
  • postal address;
  • country of residence;
  • email address;
  • telephone number;
  • date of birth, where required;
  • nationality, where required for an accommodation or legal record;
  • identity-document or passport information, where reasonably required for accommodation, security, fraud prevention or legal compliance;
  • account username and account information;
  • preferred method of communication; and
  • emergency-contact information where voluntarily provided or reasonably required.

We do not collect identity-document information merely because it is technically possible. We collect it only where it is reasonably necessary for the service, security or an applicable legal obligation.

3.2 Booking and service information

This may include:

  • dates and duration of a stay;
  • room or accommodation selected;
  • number and identity of guests;
  • arrival and departure information;
  • pool, work-lounge, meeting-room, office, café, event or facility booking details;
  • membership or pass information;
  • seating, room-layout or event requirements;
  • gift-voucher details;
  • booking history;
  • cancellations, amendments and refunds;
  • preferences communicated to us;
  • correspondence concerning a booking;
  • special requests;
  • complaints, incidents, damage reports and feedback; and
  • any notes reasonably required to deliver the requested service.

3.3 Payment and transaction information

This may include:

  • the amount paid or payable;
  • transaction date and time;
  • payment status;
  • currency;
  • invoice and receipt information;
  • partial payment-card information made available to us by the payment processor;
  • refund information;
  • chargeback or payment-dispute information; and
  • records required for bookkeeping, tax, fraud prevention and financial reconciliation.

Online card payments are processed through Stripe or another payment provider identified during checkout.

We do not ordinarily receive or store the complete payment-card number or card security code. Those details are entered into and processed through the payment provider's secure payment environment.

3.4 Parking information

Where a person uses parking operated, managed or arranged by M House, we may collect:

  • vehicle registration number;
  • vehicle description;
  • parking dates and times;
  • the associated guest, member, customer or booking; and
  • information concerning unauthorised parking, access problems, damage or incidents.

We do not collect a vehicle registration number from website visitors or customers who do not use our parking.

3.5 Communications

We may retain communications sent through:

  • email;
  • telephone;
  • website forms;
  • WhatsApp;
  • Instagram, Facebook or other social-media platforms;
  • booking platforms;
  • review platforms; and
  • in-person communications recorded by an authorised member of staff.

This may include the content of the message, contact details, relevant account name, timestamps and our response.

Telephone calls are not recorded unless we inform the caller before recording begins.

3.6 Marketing information

This may include:

  • marketing preferences;
  • evidence and date of consent;
  • email and telephone contact details;
  • previous bookings or purchases;
  • engagement with marketing messages;
  • links opened or offers viewed;
  • suppression or unsubscribe status;
  • audience or campaign identifiers used by advertising platforms; and
  • preferences inferred from a person's interaction with M House, where permitted by law.

3.7 Technical and website information

When a person uses mhouse.cy, we and our technology providers may collect:

  • IP address;
  • browser type and version;
  • device type;
  • operating system;
  • approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • language and time-zone settings;
  • pages viewed;
  • dates, times and duration of visits;
  • referring website;
  • booking or checkout journey;
  • buttons and links used;
  • error and security logs;
  • cookie and similar-technology identifiers; and
  • information necessary to prevent abuse, fraud or unauthorised access.

3.8 Guest Wi-Fi information

Where M House provides guest or member Wi-Fi, we or the network provider may process:

  • device identifiers;
  • IP and network address;
  • connection time and duration;
  • access-point information;
  • network authentication information; and
  • security, performance and abuse-prevention logs.

We do not use guest Wi-Fi to inspect the content of private communications. Network information may nevertheless be processed where necessary to operate and secure the network, investigate misuse or comply with law.

3.9 CCTV and security information

CCTV may operate in designated entrances, access points, parking areas, common areas or other appropriate locations at M House.

CCTV is used for:

  • protecting guests, staff, visitors and property;
  • preventing and investigating theft, damage and unauthorised access;
  • managing security incidents; and
  • establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

CCTV is not used in bedrooms, bathrooms, changing areas or other places where a person reasonably expects complete privacy.

Appropriate signs are displayed in monitored areas.

3.10 Photographs, video and event material

We may take or receive photographs or video relating to the property, facilities, events or promotional activities.

Where an identifiable individual is the intended subject of marketing photography or video, we will obtain an appropriate permission or release unless another lawful basis clearly applies.

People attending an event may appear incidentally in general crowd or atmosphere photography. Where practical, we will inform attendees that photography or filming is taking place.

A person may object at the time or contact us afterwards at hello@mhouse.cy.

3.11 Reviews and feedback

We may display reviews or ratings obtained from third-party platforms, including booking, travel, map or review services.

Depending on the platform and the review, this may include:

  • reviewer name or public profile name;
  • rating;
  • review text;
  • date;
  • public response; and
  • information publicly associated with the review.

We may also invite customers to provide feedback directly. Users cannot upload reviews, photographs or other public content directly onto mhouse.cy unless we introduce and expressly identify such a feature in the future.

3.12 Employment information

Where a person applies for work, we may collect:

  • name and contact details;
  • curriculum vitae;
  • employment and education history;
  • qualifications;
  • language skills;
  • references;
  • interview notes;
  • availability;
  • right-to-work information;
  • expected remuneration; and
  • other information submitted as part of the application.

Additional privacy information may be provided to successful applicants and employees.

4. Sensitive information

Some information voluntarily supplied to us may reveal health, disability, allergy, dietary or accessibility information.

We ask that customers provide only information genuinely necessary for us to deliver the requested service safely and appropriately.

Where this information constitutes special-category personal data, we process it only where:

  • the person has given explicit consent;
  • processing is necessary to protect vital interests;
  • processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims; or
  • another condition permitted by applicable law applies.

We do not use health, disability, allergy or accessibility information for advertising.

5. Sources of personal data

We may collect information:

  • directly from the person concerned;
  • from the person who makes a booking for another adult;
  • through mhouse.cy;
  • through our booking, payment, membership or property-management systems;
  • from Stripe or another payment provider;
  • from accommodation, travel, review or reservation platforms;
  • from event organisers or corporate customers;
  • from social-media and messaging services;
  • from a person's employer or organisation where it arranges the service;
  • from publicly available reviews;
  • from security systems, access systems, Wi-Fi systems and CCTV;
  • from professional advisers, insurers, payment services or authorities; and
  • from technical and analytics providers used by our website.

A person making a booking or enquiry for another adult must have authority to provide that person's information and should make this Privacy Policy available to them.

6. How and why we use personal data

6.1 To answer enquiries and take steps before a contract

We use identity, contact, enquiry and service information to:

  • respond to questions;
  • prepare quotations or proposals;
  • check availability;
  • discuss event, office or meeting requirements;
  • handle viewing requests;
  • assist with a booking journey; and
  • take requested steps before a booking or other contract is entered into.

Legal basis: taking steps at the person's request before entering into a contract and, where relevant, our legitimate interest in responding to genuine business enquiries.

6.2 To create and fulfil bookings and memberships

We use personal data to:

  • create, confirm and manage bookings;
  • provide accommodation;
  • provide pool, café, work-lounge, office, meeting-room, parking, event and other services;
  • administer memberships and passes;
  • communicate practical information;
  • manage check-in and check-out;
  • allocate rooms, facilities, seating or equipment;
  • process amendments and cancellations;
  • provide receipts and invoices; and
  • resolve service problems.

Legal basis: performance of a contract and steps requested before entering into a contract.

6.3 To process payments

We use transaction, booking and contact information to:

  • request and process payment;
  • authenticate transactions;
  • manage deposits;
  • issue receipts;
  • make refunds;
  • respond to chargebacks;
  • investigate suspected fraud; and
  • reconcile payments with bookings and accounts.

Legal basis: performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations and our legitimate interests in receiving payment and preventing fraud.

6.4 To comply with law

We may process personal data to:

  • maintain tax, VAT and accounting records;
  • comply with accommodation, employment, licensing, safety and regulatory requirements;
  • respond to lawful requests from courts, police, tax authorities, regulators or other competent authorities;
  • maintain legally required records; and
  • establish a person's identity where legally necessary.

Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation.

6.5 To operate M House safely

We use CCTV, access, parking, Wi-Fi, booking, incident and communication information to:

  • maintain security;
  • protect people and property;
  • enforce access rules;
  • respond to emergencies;
  • investigate accidents, theft, damage, misuse or complaints;
  • protect our networks and systems; and
  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Legal basis: our legitimate interests in maintaining a safe and secure property, protecting our rights and preventing unlawful activity. In an emergency, processing may also be necessary to protect vital interests.

6.6 To manage service quality

We may use booking, complaint, feedback and operational information to:

  • resolve complaints;
  • investigate service failures;
  • train staff;
  • improve procedures;
  • understand demand;
  • plan staffing and capacity;
  • evaluate facilities and services; and
  • maintain appropriate service standards.

Legal basis: our legitimate interests in operating and improving M House and fulfilling our contractual obligations.

We seek to use aggregated or anonymised information where individual identification is unnecessary.

6.7 To send marketing

Where permitted, we may send information about:

  • rooms and stays;
  • pool access and passes;
  • work-lounge and office services;
  • meeting rooms;
  • food and beverage offers;
  • events;
  • gift vouchers;
  • membership options; and
  • other M House news and promotions.

Marketing may be sent by email, WhatsApp, text message, social-media channel or another method selected by the customer.

Legal basis: consent where consent is required.

Where permitted by applicable law, we may also contact an existing customer about M House services similar to those previously purchased or requested, subject to a clear opportunity to object.

Marketing consent is optional and is not a condition of receiving a service.

Consent can be withdrawn at any time by:

  • using the unsubscribe function in a marketing message;
  • replying with an unsubscribe request where that option is available; or
  • contacting hello@mhouse.cy.

Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before consent was withdrawn.

We may retain minimal suppression information after an unsubscribe request to ensure that the person is not inadvertently added back to the same marketing list.

6.8 Advertising and audience measurement

Where a person has provided any consent required for advertising technologies, we may use analytics and advertising services to:

  • measure campaign performance;
  • understand how visitors reach and use the website;
  • show M House advertising on third-party platforms;
  • create or use matched or similar audiences;
  • limit repeated advertisements; and
  • understand whether an advertisement led to an enquiry or booking.

Providers may include Google, Meta or equivalent services selected by us from time to time.

Where customer contact data is used for an advertising audience, we will use available safeguards, such as hashing, restricted audience settings and contractual controls, and will do so only where a lawful basis exists.

6.9 To operate accounts and digital services

Where accounts or member logins are available, we process account, identity, booking and security information to:

  • register and authenticate the user;
  • display applicable bookings, passes or memberships;
  • enable account management;
  • prevent unauthorised access; and
  • provide support.

Legal basis: performance of a contract and our legitimate interest in operating secure digital services.

6.10 Recruitment

We process employment-application information to assess suitability, conduct interviews, communicate with applicants and make recruitment decisions.

Legal basis: taking steps before entering into an employment contract, compliance with legal obligations and our legitimate interest in recruiting suitable staff.

12. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, tax, accounting, safety, fraud-prevention and dispute-resolution requirements.

The following periods are our normal starting points. A record may be kept longer where a complaint, investigation, chargeback, debt, accident, legal claim or regulatory requirement is ongoing.

12.1 Enquiries that do not become bookings

General enquiries are normally retained for up to 24 months after the last meaningful communication.

Detailed event, office or commercial proposals may be retained for up to 36 months where future follow-up remains reasonably likely.

12.2 Guest, booking and service records

Operational booking and service records are normally retained for up to six years after completion or cancellation of the relevant service.

Information that is no longer necessary for operations may be reduced, archived or restricted while financial and legal records are retained.

12.3 Payment, invoice, VAT and accounting records

Transaction, invoice and accounting records are retained for at least six years after the relevant entry or transaction, or longer where required by tax, accounting, audit or legal rules.

Complete card details are retained by the payment provider in accordance with its own retention and legal obligations, rather than by M House.

12.4 Marketing records

Active marketing information is retained until consent is withdrawn, the person objects, the address becomes permanently invalid or the marketing programme ends.

Evidence of consent and suppression records may be retained for up to six years after the final marketing communication or withdrawal, where necessary to demonstrate compliance and respect an unsubscribe request.

12.5 CCTV

CCTV footage is normally retained for no longer than 30 days.

Relevant footage may be isolated and retained longer where it is needed to investigate an incident, support an insurance matter, respond to an authority or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

12.6 Wi-Fi and security logs

Routine guest Wi-Fi, access and technical security logs are normally retained for up to 12 months, unless a shorter period is sufficient or a longer period is required to investigate misuse, fraud, cyber-security events or legal claims.

12.7 Parking information

Routine parking records are normally retained for up to 12 months after the parking entitlement ends.

Information connected with unpaid charges, unauthorised parking, damage, an incident or a dispute may be retained for up to six years after resolution, or longer where legal proceedings require it.

12.8 Complaints, accidents and incidents

Complaint and service-recovery records are normally retained for up to six years after closure.

Accident, security, insurance and significant incident records may be retained for a longer period where the applicable limitation period, the age or circumstances of an affected person, an insurer or a legal obligation requires it.

12.9 Recruitment

Unsuccessful applications are normally retained for six months after the recruitment process ends.

With the applicant's express permission, details may be retained for up to 24 months for future vacancies.

Records relating to successful applicants become part of the relevant employment records.

12.10 Website analytics

Identifiable or pseudonymous analytics data is retained according to the settings configured with the current analytics provider and should normally not be retained for longer than 26 months, unless a shorter period is selected or a longer period is justified and lawfully configured.

M House does not ordinarily make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects solely through automated processing.

Payment, security or booking systems may automatically flag transactions, access attempts or bookings for fraud, availability or security reasons. Where an automated flag materially affects a person, the matter may be reviewed by M House or the relevant provider where required by law.

  • request confirmation that we process their personal data;
  • access their personal data;

These rights are not absolute. For example, we may need to retain information to comply with a legal obligation, complete a contract, protect another person's rights or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.
