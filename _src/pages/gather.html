Gather

A place to
come together

Somewhere to relax, unwind, work a little and fall into easy conversation. Above all, M House is a place to gather.

The everyday

Come as you are,
stay as long as you like.

A Mediterranean courtyard, a saltwater pool and easy lounges, open to guests and visitors alike. Spend a slow afternoon by the water, work casually with a coffee in the shade, meet a friend, or meet someone new. Locals and travellers, side by side. The house does the rest.

A daily ritual

Aperitivo,
every evening.

Inspired by the Italian tradition of gathering before dinner, Aperitivo is our daily social ritual. Meet by the pool, enjoy a drink and complimentary bites, and watch the afternoon turn into evening. Arrive alone or with friends. Every day, 17:00 to 19:00. Open to all, walk-ins welcome.

And there is always something on

Small gatherings,
an added pleasure.

Beyond the everyday, we host a handful of gatherings that bring locals and visitors together through food, culture and wellness. A welcome extra, never the whole point. By advance booking.

01

Yaya's Corner

Experience Cyprus through the eyes of local Cypriot women as they share seasonal recipes, stories and traditions passed down through generations. Includes wine, beer, water and snacks.

2 to 3 hours · max 12 · €50 pp
02

Breakfast and Swim

Start the day with a refreshing swim followed by a traditional Cypriot breakfast known as Boukoma. Includes pool access and breakfast.

About 2 hours · max 20 · €25 pp
03

Sunrise Yoga

Start the morning with a guided yoga session before enjoying access to the pool. Includes pool access.

About 1 hour · max 12 · €20 pp
04

Water Yoga

A refreshing yoga experience combining movement and mindfulness in and around the pool. Includes pool access.

About 1 hour · max 12 · €20 pp

Seasonal gatherings and experiences are introduced throughout the year. All gatherings are available by advance booking.
