If we discover that personal data has been submitted by or concerning a person under 18 without a valid legal reason, we will delete or restrict that information as appropriate.

12. Data retention
We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, tax, accounting, safety, fraud-prevention and dispute-resolution requirements.
The following periods are our normal starting points. A record may be kept longer where a complaint, investigation, chargeback, debt, accident, legal claim or regulatory requirement is ongoing.
12.1 Enquiries that do not become bookings
General enquiries are normally retained for up to 24 months after the last meaningful communication.
Detailed event, office or commercial proposals may be retained for up to 36 months where future follow-up remains reasonably likely.
12.2 Guest, booking and service records
Operational booking and service records are normally retained for up to six years after completion or cancellation of the relevant service.
Information that is no longer necessary for operations may be reduced, archived or restricted while financial and legal records are retained.
12.3 Payment, invoice, VAT and accounting records
Transaction, invoice and accounting records are retained for at least six years after the relevant entry or transaction, or longer where required by tax, accounting, audit or legal rules.
Complete card details are retained by the payment provider in accordance with its own retention and legal obligations, rather than by M House.
12.4 Marketing records
Active marketing information is retained until consent is withdrawn, the person objects, the address becomes permanently invalid or the marketing programme ends.
Evidence of consent and suppression records may be retained for up to six years after the final marketing communication or withdrawal, where necessary to demonstrate compliance and respect an unsubscribe request.
12.5 CCTV
CCTV footage is normally retained for no longer than 30 days.
Relevant footage may be isolated and retained longer where it is needed to investigate an incident, support an insurance matter, respond to an authority or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.
12.6 Wi-Fi and security logs
Routine guest Wi-Fi, access and technical security logs are normally retained for up to 12 months, unless a shorter period is sufficient or a longer period is required to investigate misuse, fraud, cyber-security events or legal claims.
12.7 Parking information
Routine parking records are normally retained for up to 12 months after the parking entitlement ends.
Information connected with unpaid charges, unauthorised parking, damage, an incident or a dispute may be retained for up to six years after resolution, or longer where legal proceedings require it.
12.8 Complaints, accidents and incidents
Complaint and service-recovery records are normally retained for up to six years after closure.
Accident, security, insurance and significant incident records may be retained for a longer period where the applicable limitation period, the age or circumstances of an affected person, an insurer or a legal obligation requires it.
12.9 Recruitment
Unsuccessful applications are normally retained for six months after the recruitment process ends.
With the applicant's express permission, details may be retained for up to 24 months for future vacancies.
Records relating to successful applicants become part of the relevant employment records.
12.10 Website analytics
Identifiable or pseudonymous analytics data is retained according to the settings configured with the current analytics provider and should normally not be retained for longer than 26 months, unless a shorter period is selected or a longer period is justified and lawfully configured.
